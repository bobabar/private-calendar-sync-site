Terms of use

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Private Calendar Sync is a personal utility for its owner’s accounts. This website explains the utility and its data practices. It does not offer a public hosted synchronization service, account registration or paid subscription.

Authorized use

Use the application only with accounts and calendars you own or are authorized to access. Microsoft and Google account terms and applicable API policies continue to apply. Authorization can be revoked at any time.

How synchronization works

The app mirrors Outlook events into a separate Google Calendar. Outlook remains the place to accept or decline invitations and edit original events. Edits to mirrored Google events do not update Outlook and may be overwritten by a later sync.

Synchronization depends on the device being available, valid account authorization, network connectivity and the providers’ APIs. It may be delayed or interrupted. Check the original Outlook invitation for time-sensitive details and meeting changes.

Availability

The utility is provided as is, without a guarantee of uninterrupted operation, accuracy or fitness for a particular purpose. It is not a backup service. Keep access to the original calendar and review important appointments there. Nothing in these terms excludes rights or liabilities that cannot legally be excluded.

Privacy and ending use

The privacy policy explains the data accessed, stored and transferred. To stop using the application, stop its background task and revoke its access. Previously copied events remain unless removed separately.
