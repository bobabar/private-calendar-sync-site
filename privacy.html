Privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Private Calendar Sync is a personal utility operated by its owner to mirror their Outlook calendar into a separate Google Calendar. This policy describes the local application and this informational website.

Data the app accesses

After the owner authorizes each account, the app accesses Microsoft account identifiers and Outlook calendar events. Event data can include titles, descriptions, start and end times, time zones, locations, meeting links, recurrence, availability, cancellation status and the owner’s response status.

Google access identifies the authorized account and allows the app to create secondary calendars and see, create, change and delete events on calendars it creates. The app uses that access for its dedicated Outlook mirror calendar.

How data is used and shared

The app copies event titles, times, locations, descriptions and meeting links from Microsoft to the owner’s Google Calendar, and reconciles changes and removals. Event descriptions may themselves contain personal information supplied by meeting organizers. Account identifiers are used to check that the intended accounts are connected.

Synchronization runs on the authorized device and communicates directly with Microsoft and Google over HTTPS. The app does not sell data, use it for advertising, send it to AI models or provide it to other third-party services. Microsoft and Google process data under their own policies.

The app creates a private destination calendar and private events. It does not publish a calendar feed, copy attendee lists into Google invitations or send invitations. Changes the owner later makes to calendar sharing can affect who can view the copied information.

Storage and retention

OAuth access and refresh credentials are stored in the operating system’s credential store. The app does not collect account passwords. Account identifiers are stored with those credentials. A local SQLite database stores synchronization metadata, including event identifiers, hashes, dates and run status; it is not a full archive of event descriptions.

Copied events remain in Google Calendar until removed by synchronization or by the owner. Local metadata remains until the owner deletes it. The app uses a configured date window, so copies outside that window may remain until manually removed. Provider storage and backups are governed by the provider’s policies.

Disconnecting and deleting data

The owner can stop the background task, run the app’s disconnect command to remove its stored credentials, and revoke access in their Google and Microsoft account settings. Disconnecting does not delete previously copied events. To remove those copies, delete the dedicated Google calendar or its events. The owner can separately delete the local app data folder and any backups.

This website

This static website has no account sign-in, calendar connection, analytics scripts, advertising, forms or application cookies. It does not receive calendar events or OAuth tokens. Its hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, requested URLs and browser details, to deliver and protect the site.

Google API data use

Private Calendar Sync’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Policy changes

This is a personal utility with no public registration. Material changes to its data practices will be reflected here with an updated date.
